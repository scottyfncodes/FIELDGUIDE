Don't give aspirin or any medication unless it's their own prescribed medication or a dispatcher tells you to.
Use an epinephrine auto-injector immediately if available - inject into the outer thigh.
Rolling boil 1 min (3 min above 6,500 ft / 2,000 m).
Never cross moving water
Don't put anything in their mouth
Only rewarm in warm - not hot - water, around 100-105°F (37-40°C), for 15-30 minutes
Don't induce vomiting unless specifically told to by Poison Control or a medical professional
